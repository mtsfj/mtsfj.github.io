A sprite sheet of karate poses, to be used as the shadows of a karate class cast on the lit paper of a Japanese dojo's shoji doors. Follow the attached guide exactly: 4 rows of 6 cells, one figure per cell, keep the thin cell borders and the floor line in each cell. In every cell replace the grey mannequin by the silhouette of the same adult karateka in exactly the mannequin's pose, at exactly the mannequin's size and place: the feet (or the knees and shins when kneeling) on the cell's floor line, the head where the mannequin's head is. All 24 figures at the same scale. The pale limbs of a mannequin are the far arm and the far leg, partly hidden behind the body.

The same person in every cell: an adult of average build with short hair, barefoot, in a traditional karate gi: a heavy cotton jacket with wide sleeves ending at mid-forearm and a skirt that covers the hips, loose trousers ending above the ankles, and a belt wrapped twice round the waist and tied in a square knot in front, its two ends hanging down to mid-thigh. In side views the sleeves' openings, the jacket's skirt, the belt's knot and its hanging ends must read in the outline. Hands are fists unless said otherwise.

Each figure is one pure solid silhouette: a single flat black tone (#111111) with no inner detail, no outline, no rim light, no gradient, no grey, no floor shadow, on the guide's flat pale background. No text, no numbers, nothing else in the cells. Strict side profile for the side views (facing right), as in the guide.

Row 1, left to right: 1 standing at attention, heels together, open hands flat on the front of the thighs; 2 the standing bow (rei), the back straight, bent forward about 30 degrees from the hips, open hands on the thighs; 3 yoi, the ready stance, feet apart, both fists held just in front of the belt; 4 choku-zuki from a parallel stance: the near arm punching straight forward at chest height, the far fist pulled back to the hip; 5 front view, facing the viewer, in a front stance, a straight punch coming straight at the viewer (the fist in front of the chest's middle), the other fist pulled back to the hip, its elbow showing behind; 6 kneeling in seiza, sitting upright on the heels, open hands on the thighs.

Row 2, left to right (Heian Shodan's techniques): 1 gedan-barai in zenkutsu-dachi (long front stance, front knee bent, back leg straight): the blocking arm straight down and forward over the front knee, the other fist at the hip; 2 oi-zuki in zenkutsu-dachi: the arm on the front leg's side punching straight forward at solar plexus height, the other fist at the hip; 3 age-uke in zenkutsu-dachi: the blocking forearm raised just above and in front of the forehead, slanting up, the other fist at the hip; 4 shuto-uke in kokutsu-dachi (back stance, the weight on the bent back leg): the front hand open as a knife hand, edge forward at chest height, the back hand open, palm up, in front of the solar plexus; 5 tettsui-uchi: feet close together, the near arm striking forward with the bottom of the fist at face height; 6 mae-geri: standing on the far leg, the near leg kicking straight forward at belt height, the toes pulled back to strike with the ball of the foot, the arms in guard.

Row 3, left to right (the moments between): 1 a stepping oi-zuki half way, the feet passing each other, knees bent, the punching arm still extended; 2 a step with the blocking fist raised by the ear before a gedan-barai, the other arm reaching down and forward; 3 a step with the arms crossing in front of the face before an age-uke; 4 a step with the open knife hand raised by the ear before a shuto-uke, the other open hand reaching down and forward; 5 the knee raised high before the front kick, the foot tucked under the knee; 6 the bow from seiza: kneeling, the back bent forward, both hands flat on the floor, the head low.

Row 4, left to right: 1 and 2 the sensei walking slowly to the right, upright, hands clasped behind his back (two steps of a walk); 3 the sensei standing, upright, arms folded on his chest; 4 front view, facing the viewer, yoi: feet apart, fists in front of the belt; 5 front view, facing the viewer, in a front stance: gedan-barai, the figure's left arm straight down and out to the side over its knee, the right fist at the hip; 6 front view, facing the viewer, in a front stance: age-uke, the figure's left forearm raised above the forehead, slanting up across it, the right fist at the hip.
