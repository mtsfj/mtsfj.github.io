This is an enlarged crop (each painting pixel is a 6 x 6 block) of a pixel-art painting of a narrow Japanese residential street at night. These houses are on the right-hand side of the street: their fronts recede toward a vanishing point far to the LEFT, out of the frame, so their horizontal lines (ledges, eaves, the foot of the walls on the sidewalk) slope gently toward the left. A dark concrete utility pole stands in front, in the middle, with a red sign on it.

Redraw only the two lit entrances, which are illegible blotches now. Everything else stays exactly as it is, pixel for pixel: the pole and its sign, the upper floors, the dark walls, the ramen shop on the right (its dark plaster wall, its framed menu board, its lit doors), the sidewalk.

1. Right of the pole, the entrance of a small Japanese house (genkan), recessed a little into its dark wall. Keep its outline, place and size to the pixel. From top to bottom: the small corrugated metal awning (its ribs even, its front edge thin and straight); under it a frosted glass transom lit from inside, with the small ceiling lamp of the porch; the door frame (thin painted aluminium); on the left of the door a narrow frosted glass sidelight glowing softly pink-violet from the hall light inside; the door leaf, a plain dark door of a Japanese house, with a small frosted window and a lever handle at the places shown; at the bottom the concrete step, its front edge on the sidewalk. The door is seen almost face on, as painted. Inside its recess only its left side shows, catching the light; on its right the frame meets the ramen shop's wall in one clean straight dark shadow line, from the awning down to the sidewalk.

2. Left of the pole, the lit entrance hall of the small apartment building, seen at an angle, its right part hidden behind the pole (it goes on behind it, nothing is squeezed in). It is lit pink-violet by a fluorescent ceiling light: on its back wall a small wall lamp, an intercom panel, and below them a column of small metal mailboxes; a thin pipe runs up the wall above. Keep their places and sizes.

Keep the painter's palette and light: deep night blues, the pink-violet glow of the two entrances, dark walls. Same pixel size, crisp pixels, clean straight edges, no speckle noise, no blur. Do not add any text, person or object.
